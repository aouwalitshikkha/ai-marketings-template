ChatGPT for Digital Marketers

Sarah Chen AI Marketing Specialist
| ★★★★★ 4.9 (128 reviews)

Learn how to leverage ChatGPT for every aspect of digital marketing. From content ideation and copywriting to email campaigns and audience research, this hands-on course teaches you practical workflows that save hours every week.

You will learn by doing. Each module includes real-world exercises, ready-to-use prompts, and templates you can immediately apply to your marketing projects. By the end, you will have a complete AI-powered marketing toolkit.

Prerequisites: No prior AI experience needed. Basic familiarity with digital marketing concepts is helpful but not required.

What You'll Learn

Write effective ChatGPT prompts for marketing tasks
Generate email sequences, landing pages, and ad copy
Conduct audience research and competitor analysis with AI
Create social media content calendars in minutes
Analyze and optimize marketing performance using AI insights
Build reusable prompt libraries for your marketing team

Course Curriculum

12 lessons · 4 modules

Module 1: Getting Started with ChatGPT

3 lessons · 45 min

  • What is ChatGPT and how does it work?
  • Setting up your account and choosing the right plan
  • Understanding GPT-4o capabilities and limitations

Module 2: Crafting Effective Prompts

3 lessons · 1 hour

  • Prompt anatomy: instructions, context, and format control
  • Controlling tone, audience, and brand voice
  • Advanced techniques: chain-of-thought and persona prompting

Module 3: Content Creation Workflows

3 lessons · 1.5 hours

  • Blog posts, articles, and long-form content with outlines
  • Email sequences, landing pages, and ad copy generation
  • Social media content calendars and platform-specific copy

Module 4: Marketing Automation and Analysis

3 lessons · 45 min

  • Audience research and persona development with ChatGPT
  • Competitor analysis and market research workflows
  • Analyzing campaign performance and generating AI insights
